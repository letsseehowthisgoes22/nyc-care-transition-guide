New York City

Check NYC outpatient mental health coverage when your insurance changes

If insurance changes soon after discharge, match the plan dates to the exact treatment dates. A transition can affect which office you can use, what the provider needs to submit and what you may pay for scheduled visits.

Jump to the checklist

Name both plans and verify the exact switch date

Ask the previous and new insurers when coverage ends and begins. Give the clinic the updated plan information and ask its billing desk to check the specific service and address with each plan.

  • Ask whether authorization must be requested again when plan information changes.
  • Save confirmation numbers and the date you received the information.

Discuss continuity before canceling appointments

If the provider is out of network under the new plan, ask about the cost to continue, a reimbursement process and alternatives the treating clinician can coordinate. Set a deadline to decide before the next scheduled visit.

  • Confirm which charges will be billed to which plan and who tracks pending approvals.
  • Ask whom to contact if the coverage check remains unresolved near an appointment.

Common questions

Will my existing authorization move to the new insurance?

Ask the new insurer and the provider's billing desk whether another authorization is required for the exact service.
