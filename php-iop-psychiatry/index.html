Compare PHP, IOP and outpatient psychiatry after discharge in NYC

Make the first search specific to the recommended treatment setting. A partial hospital program (PHP), intensive outpatient program (IOP) and private psychiatry office each organize the week differently. Compare the proposed schedule, staffing and admission process with the discharge team's recommendation.

Jump to the checklist

Partial hospitalization means structured care during the day

Ask for the planned days and hours and the expected start after evaluation. Have the discharge team explain what it wants the next program to provide, then ask intake whether its team delivers that support, how medication review works and which clinician coordinates with the current team.

A hospital-based program may use its own intake process even when the patient was treated elsewhere. Ask whether the current unit can send the referral before discharge, what documents the receiving team needs and whether an intake appointment establishes the start date.

Intensive outpatient schedules vary by track and age group

Ask an IOP for the specific weekly schedule and age cohort. A program designed for people ages 18–30, for example, may have a different peer group and enrollment window from a general adult day service. Ask how many hours consist of individual appointments, what happens during group hours, and whether an outside therapist or prescriber would stay involved.

Check when a second track change might occur and who decides it. If school or work must fit around treatment, show intake those constraints and ask which commitments can be accommodated within the proposed schedule.

Private outpatient psychiatry starts with a defined clinical visit

A prescriber's office may book an assessment first and set follow-up afterward. Ask whether the assessment includes medication review, what records the clinician needs and who handles instructions before the visit. If a therapist or group is also part of the discharge plan, ask which provider is arranging that separate appointment.

For any option, confirm who bills for the assessment and treatment, which services require insurance authorization, and what would be due before the first appointment. If using a superbill, ask when it is issued and confirm possible reimbursement with the specific insurance plan.

Common questions

Can I book a private medication visit while a day program reviews the referral?

Discuss that option with the discharge clinician and both receiving offices. Ask who would manage medication during the overlap and whether an outside appointment affects the program's intake or payment arrangement.

Can the first visit be virtual if I am temporarily staying outside New York?

Tell the practice where the patient will physically be at the time of the appointment. Confirm that the assigned clinician can see them there and that the program allows virtual visits for the scheduled service.
