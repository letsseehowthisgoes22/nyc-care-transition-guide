Psychiatric discharge checklist for the first week at home

The hospital and outpatient office each have part of the plan. Use these fields with the discharge clinician and receiving office to make sure a referral has reached the right person and the patient can find their next steps.

Jump to the checklist

Before departure, read back the appointment and contact details

Ask one person on the discharge team to walk through the printed or portal instructions with the patient. Confirm the outpatient provider name, date, time, office suite or video link, and the number to call if the office cancels. Separate confirmed details from questions the outpatient team still has to answer.

A completed handoff might say that Tuesday at 10 a.m. is booked for a program intake. On Monday the patient will call its admissions desk to confirm the transfer summary arrived. If the start date is still open after intake, the discharge coordinator has agreed to discuss interim follow-up by Wednesday.

  • Which visit was booked, and who at the receiving office confirmed it?
  • Who at the hospital will check a pending referral, and by what date?
  • When should the patient call if they hear nothing?

Confirm pharmacy pickup and the interim clinical contact

Review the discharge medication list with the treating team and ask which pharmacy received any new prescriptions. Ask the pharmacy about pickup hours and availability. If there is a delay, contact the clinical team indicated in the discharge plan before changing the instructions yourself. Ask whom to reach for a prescribing question before the first outpatient visit.

Give pharmacy and appointment information to the people the patient has chosen to help. Ask the providers which discharge documents they need, how to send them securely and when the receiving office can confirm receipt.

Agree on the backup before you need it

Ask the discharge team what to do if the clinic declines the referral, the pharmacy cannot fill a needed prescription or the video link fails. Write down the callback number and who will follow up, along with the team's instructions for urgent questions. If a different borough or home address makes the original plan impractical, ask about alternate appointment locations early.

Keep the plan where the patient can open it quickly. If they want help, one person can track transportation and a different person can track clinic callbacks. The patient should have the same appointment details and know who is doing each task.

Common questions

Is a referral the same as a booked appointment?

A referral asks the receiving provider to consider the patient. Verify the date and time with that office, what the visit covers and whether the clinic has accepted the patient for ongoing follow-up.

What can a family member do without handling the clinical record?

With the patient's agreement, a family member can help log callback dates, arrange a ride or pick up a prescription. Let the treating offices exchange clinical documents using their designated process.
